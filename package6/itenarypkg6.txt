🌟 Exciting Getaway to Delhi, Agra & Jaipur
Description:
Embark on a 4-night, 5-day adventure across the iconic cities of Delhi, Agra, and Jaipur. Experience the rich history, stunning architecture, and vibrant culture of India's Golden Triangle. From exploring Delhi's historical landmarks to marveling at the Taj Mahal in Agra and the grandeur of Jaipur's palaces, this customizable trip offers a perfect blend of sightseeing, relaxation, and exploration.

🏨 Hotels & Destinations:
Destination 1: New Delhi

Hotel Name: The Imperial New Delhi

Location: Janpath, Connaught Place, New Delhi

Category: Luxury 5-Star

Stay Duration: 2 Nights (Day 1 & Day 2)

Destination 2: Agra

Hotel Name: Taj View Hotel

Location: Fatehabad Road, Agra

Category: Deluxe Room

Stay Duration: 1 Night (Day 3)

Destination 3: Jaipur

Hotel Name: The Raj Palace

Location: Amer Road, Jaipur

Category: Heritage Suite

Stay Duration: 1 Night (Day 4)

🗓 Itinerary Overview:
Day 1: Arrival in New Delhi 🇮🇳
Arrival: Reach New Delhi (Airport/Railway Station) on your own

Transfer: Private AC car to The Imperial New Delhi

Accommodation: Stay at The Imperial New Delhi (Luxury 5-Star)

Activities:

🏛️ Visit India Gate and Raj Ghat

🕌 Explore Jama Masjid and Red Fort

Meals: Dinner at the hotel

Leisure Time: Evening at Connaught Place

Day 2: New Delhi Sightseeing 🏙️
Meals Included: Breakfast at the hotel

Activities:

🕌 Visit Qutub Minar

🏛️ Explore Humayun’s Tomb and Lotus Temple

🏙️ Drive through Chandni Chowk and visit Jama Masjid

Leisure Time: Shopping at Dilli Haat or Janpath Market

Meals: Breakfast at the hotel, Dinner at a local restaurant

Day 3: New Delhi to Agra 🕌
Meals Included: Breakfast at The Imperial New Delhi

Transfer: Private car to Agra (Approx. 3-4 hours)

Accommodation: Taj View Hotel (Deluxe Room)

Activities:

🏰 Visit the Taj Mahal (Sunrise if possible)

🏛️ Explore Agra Fort

🌷 Visit Mehtab Bagh for a sunset view of the Taj

Meals: Breakfast at the hotel, Dinner at a local restaurant

Day 4: Agra to Jaipur 🚗
Meals Included: Breakfast at Taj View Hotel

Transfer: Private car to Jaipur (Approx. 4-5 hours)

Accommodation: The Raj Palace (Heritage Suite)

Activities:

🏰 Visit Amer Fort

🏛️ Explore City Palace and Jantar Mantar

🕌 Visit the Hawa Mahal (Palace of Winds)

Meals: Breakfast at the hotel, Dinner at the hotel

Day 5: Jaipur Departure ✈️
Meals Included: Breakfast at The Raj Palace

Transfer: Private car to Jaipur Airport/Railway Station for departure

Activities:

🏙️ If time permits, visit Jal Mahal or enjoy a heritage walk

Meals: Breakfast at the hotel
